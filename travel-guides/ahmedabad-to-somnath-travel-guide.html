Ahmedabad to Somnath Travel Guide

Highway routes, estimated travel time, Somnath Jyotirlinga darshan tips, and nearby Gir and Junagadh detours.

Distance & Highway Options

Somnath (Prabhas Patan, near Veraval) is approximately 405–415 km from Ahmedabad. The most reliable and fastest highway corridor goes via Rajkot and Junagadh (NH 47 & NH 27 / NH 51), taking roughly 7.5 to 8 hours by cab.

Recommended Route: Ahmedabad → Bagodra → Limbdi → Rajkot → Gondal → Jetpur → Junagadh Bypass → Keshod → Veraval / Somnath.

What to Plan in Somnath

  • Somnath Mahadev Temple: Open daily from early morning (around 6:00 AM) to 10:00 PM, with morning, noon, and evening aarti sessions plus the evening light-and-sound show.
  • Bhalka Tirth & Triveni Sangam: Both are within 5 km of the main temple and easy to cover in a single morning or afternoon circuit.
  • Sasan Gir (Devalia Safari Park): Roughly 45–50 km from Somnath; ideal as an overnight or next-morning wildlife stop (note: Gir safaris require advance online permit booking).

Rajkot to Somnath Connections

If you are arriving at Rajkot by train or Hirasar Airport, Somnath is a shorter ~195 km (3.5 to 4 hour) drive south via Gondal and Jetpur. See our Rajkot to Somnath cab page for direct pickup options.
