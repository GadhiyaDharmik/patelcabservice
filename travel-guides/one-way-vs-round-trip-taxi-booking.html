One-Way vs Round-Trip Taxi Booking

How fixed one-way cab drops and per-kilometer round-trip packages work—and which one saves you more money.

When to Book a One-Way Cab

A one-way cab is priced as a point-to-point route package (for example, Ahmedabad to Rajkot starting from ₹2,499 for a sedan). It makes the most sense when:

  • You are catching a flight or train from another city and do not need a return ride.
  • You are staying at your destination for several days and don't want to pay daily vehicle retention charges.
  • You are traveling on a high-frequency corridor (Ahmedabad–Rajkot, Ahmedabad–Vadodara, Ahmedabad–Surat).

When to Book a Round-Trip Cab

A round-trip cab is billed per kilometer (e.g., ₹12/km for sedans, ₹16/km for Ertiga, ₹20/km for Innova Crysta) with the same car and driver staying with you. Choose round-trip when:

  • You are doing a same-day business visit or family function and returning the same evening.
  • You are visiting multiple sightseeing spots or temples (e.g., Dwarka–Nageshwar–Bet Dwarka or Somnath–Gir) where local transport would otherwise be inconvenient.
Tip: Before confirming a multi-day round trip, ask our team about the daily minimum kilometer slab (commonly 250–300 km/day) and driver allowance so you can compare the exact total against two separate one-way bookings.
