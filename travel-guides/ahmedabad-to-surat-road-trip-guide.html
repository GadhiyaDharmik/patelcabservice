Ahmedabad to Surat Road Trip Guide

Navigating the Ahmedabad–Vadodara Expressway (NE 1) and NH 48 smoothly for business and family trips to Surat.

Expressway Route & Travel Time

The road distance between Ahmedabad and Surat is approximately 265–285 km depending on your pickup and drop neighborhoods. The fastest route uses the Mahatma Gandhi Expressway (NE 1) from Ahmedabad to Vadodara, then joins National Highway 48 (NH 48) south through Bharuch and Ankleshwar into Surat.

Typical driving time is 4.5 to 5 hours, though industrial truck traffic around Bharuch and Kamrej can add 20–30 minutes during evening peak hours.

Corridor Breakdown: Ahmedabad (CTM / Ring Road) → Nadiad → Anand → Vadodara (NE 1 Exit) → Karjan → Bharuch → Ankleshwar → Kamrej → Surat.

Practical Tips for Business & Family Travelers

  • NE 1 Expressway Rules: Two-wheelers and slow agricultural vehicles are restricted on NE 1, making it a smooth, predictable 93 km stretch to Vadodara.
  • Popular Stops: Food plazas near Anand/Vadodara and clean highway restaurants near Karjan or Ankleshwar offer quick refreshment breaks.
  • Surat Drop Zones: Specify whether you are heading to Varachha, Ring Road Textile Market, Adajan, Vesu, Piplod, or Surat Airport so your driver picks the best entry point (Kamrej vs Kadodara).

One-Way vs Same-Day Return

For textile or diamond business visits where you return the same evening, a round-trip cab lets you keep the vehicle on standby across Surat. For one-way airport or relocation drops, see our Ahmedabad to Surat cab page.
