How to Choose the Right Taxi for Outstation Travel

Match your passenger count, luggage volume, and highway distance to the right cab category without overpaying.

1. Sedans: Swift Dzire & Hyundai Aura (₹12/km Round Trip)

Best for: 1 to 4 passengers with 2 medium suitcases or 3 cabin bags. Ideal for Ahmedabad–Rajkot, Ahmedabad–Vadodara, or airport transfers for couples and solo business travelers.

2. Family MPV: Maruti Ertiga (₹16/km Round Trip)

Best for: 4 to 6 passengers. Offers a higher seating position and rear AC vents, making it popular for family trips to Dwarka, Somnath, or Surat. If you have 6 passengers plus heavy luggage, ask for a roof carrier in advance.

3. Executive MPV: Toyota Innova Crysta (₹20/km Round Trip)

Best for: Long 400+ km highway drives, senior citizens, or corporate travelers who want extra legroom, suspension comfort, and strong AC cooling across multi-day tours.

Quick Decision Checklist

  • Count large check-in suitcases (especially for international airport pickups), not just passengers.
  • For trips over 6 hours, prioritize legroom and rear AC vents.
  • Always confirm the exact car model and AC availability when locking your quote.
