Ahmedabad to Rajkot Taxi Service – Complete Travel Guide

Everything you need to plan a smooth road trip between Ahmedabad and Rajkot: highway route, distance, timing, stops, and cab options.

Distance, Highway & Travel Time

The road distance between Ahmedabad and Rajkot is approximately 215 km via National Highway 47 (NH 47). Under normal highway traffic, a private cab covers the stretch in 4.5 to 5 hours, including a brief tea or meal break.

If your pickup is in eastern Ahmedabad (such as Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport or Kalupur Railway Station), exiting via Sardar Patel Ring Road toward Sarkhej–Bavla adds 20–35 minutes depending on city traffic.

Quick Route Summary: Ahmedabad → Sarkhej / Sanand Crossroad → Bavla → Bagodra → Limbdi → Sayla → Chotila → Bamanbore → Greenland Circle, Rajkot.

Useful Stops Along NH 47

  • Bagodra & Limbdi: Convenient midway points with highway restaurants, clean washrooms, and fuel stations.
  • Sayla: Popular for quick Kathiyawadi snacks and tea breaks roughly halfway through the journey.
  • Chotila (Chamunda Mata Temple): Located about 50 km before Rajkot. Many families request a short darshan halt here; if you plan to climb the hill temple, allow 60–90 extra minutes in your schedule.

Best Time to Travel

Early morning departures (between 5:30 AM and 7:30 AM) help you clear Ahmedabad city limits before peak office traffic and reach Rajkot before midday. Evening departures are also common for passengers landing at Ahmedabad Airport (AMD).

Choosing One-Way vs Round-Trip

For a single drop to Rajkot city or Hirasar Airport, a one-way Ahmedabad to Rajkot cab (starting from ₹2,499 for sedans) is usually more economical than paying two-way per-kilometer charges. If you are returning the same day or next day, compare one-way vs round-trip pricing before confirming.
