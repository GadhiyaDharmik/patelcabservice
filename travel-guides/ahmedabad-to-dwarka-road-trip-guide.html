Ahmedabad to Dwarka Road Trip Guide

Distance, highway route via Rajkot and Jamnagar, temple darshan planning, and practical tips for a comfortable Saurashtra pilgrimage drive.

Route, Distance & Driving Time

Dwarka lies on Gujarat's westernmost tip in Devbhumi Dwarka district, roughly 440–455 km from Ahmedabad by road. Most travelers take NH 47 to Rajkot, followed by the Rajkot–Jamnagar–Khambhalia–Dwarka corridor (around 7.5 to 8.5 hours with meal breaks).

Primary Highway Route: Ahmedabad → Bagodra → Limbdi → Rajkot → Dhrol → Jamnagar Bypass → Khambhalia → Dwarka.

Key Places to Cover in Dwarka

  • Dwarkadhish Temple (Jagat Mandir): Plan your arrival around morning (6:30 AM–1:00 PM) or evening (5:00 PM–9:30 PM) darshan windows, as the temple closes in the afternoon.
  • Nageshwar Jyotirlinga: Located about 17 km north of Dwarka town along the road toward Okha.
  • Bet Dwarka: Accessible via Okha (around 30 km from Dwarka) by boat/ferry or the Sudarshan Setu cable-stayed bridge; allow half a day for this excursion.
  • Rukmini Devi Temple & Gomti Ghat: Easy local stops within 2–3 km of the main temple.

Planning Your Cab & Itinerary

Because the one-way drive is nearly 8 hours, families often prefer a 6+1 seater Ertiga or Innova Crysta for extra legroom. Many travelers also combine Dwarka with Somnath via Porbandar (the coastal NH 51 stretch) as a 3-to-4-day round-trip circuit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to book a one-way or round-trip cab for Dwarka?

If you are returning to Ahmedabad within 2–4 days or continuing to Somnath, a round-trip cab keeps the same vehicle with you for local temple visits around Dwarka, Nageshwar, and Okha.

When is the best season to visit Dwarka?

October to March offers pleasant coastal weather. Monsoon (July–September) brings greenery but occasional heavy rain, while summers (April–June) are hot during midday.
