1. Booking & Confirmation

All cab bookings with Patel Cab Service are confirmed via phone call or WhatsApp (+91 98796-77359) after reviewing pickup location, drop destination, travel date, vehicle category, and fare structure. 2. Fare Structure & Extra Charges

  • One-Way Trips: Quoted as a route-specific package between the agreed pickup and drop points. Additional pickups, detours, or extra waiting beyond the agreed grace period may incur extra charges.
  • Round-Trip & Outstation Trips: Billed on a per-kilometer basis according to the selected vehicle category (e.g., Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta), subject to minimum daily kilometer terms and driver allowance where applicable, which are communicated prior to booking.
  • Tolls, Parking & State Taxes: Highway tolls, airport parking fees, and interstate border taxes are payable by the passenger at actuals unless an all-inclusive quote is explicitly agreed in writing/WhatsApp.

3. Passenger Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate pickup address, contact number, and flight/train details for airport or railway station pickups.
  • Carry luggage appropriate to the booked vehicle's boot and seating capacity.
  • Refrain from carrying illegal goods, hazardous materials, or exceeding the permitted passenger seating limit of the vehicle.
