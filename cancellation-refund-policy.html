Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Patel Cab Service

1. How to Cancel or Reschedule

If your travel plans change, please inform our dispatch team as soon as possible by calling +91 98796-77359 or messaging us on WhatsApp with your booking name, pickup city, and scheduled date/time.

2. Advance Notice & Cancellation Terms

  • No Advance Paid: Most standard one-way and outstation inquiries confirmed without an advance deposit can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge with prior notice before driver dispatch.
  • Bookings With Advance Token: Where an advance token was paid to reserve a specific vehicle (such as peak season, multi-day tours, or Innova Crysta bookings), any applicable cancellation or rescheduling terms are shared at the time of booking confirmation.
  • After Driver Dispatch: If a vehicle has already been dispatched to your pickup point (such as an airport or outstation pickup), reasonable dead-mileage or waiting charges may apply.

3. Flight or Train Delays

For Ahmedabad Airport (AMD) or railway station pickups, please notify us immediately if your flight or train is delayed so we can adjust your pickup schedule where possible without cancelling the ride.

4. Refund Processing

Approved refunds for advance payments are processed back via the original payment method (UPI or bank transfer). Please allow standard banking processing timelines and contact patelcabservice@gmail.com or +91 98796-77359 for status updates.
